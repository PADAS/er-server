# Camera Traps Requirements
## Summary
Provide scientists using camera traps in their research a platform to track and record maintenance of these deployments.

## Product should have
1. Deployment - location, time/date, location id, camera id, height of camera, distance to target, signoff camera was configured and doublec checked. Settings to confirm are: battery level, image format, shot setting (continous, three)
   1. After deployment is uploaded to ER, show on map where this deployment is located
   2. Show some of the deployment information on card.
   3. Link to show all events associated with this deployment. Includes deployment event + revisit events
2. Revisit event - time/date, state of battery on arrival and exit, camera, recalibrate any of the original settings of height, distance to target. Was battery replaced.
